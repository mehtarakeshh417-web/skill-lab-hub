CSS variables:
- Dark: `--primary: oklch(0.55 0.20 25)`, `--primary-glow: oklch(0.70 0.22 30)`, `--ring: oklch(0.62 0.18 25)`
- Light: `--primary: oklch(0.55 0.18 25)`, `--primary-glow: oklch(0.70 0.20 30)`, `--ring: oklch(0.55 0.18 25)`

Success states may remain emerald green for semantic clarity. Technology logo brand colors stay official.
